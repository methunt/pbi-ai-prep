## Verdict

**42 of 61 checked decision-clusters landed; 19 dropped.** The token system (colors, type, radii, shadows), component specs, behavioral contract, and product-critical microcopy landed essentially verbatim. Drops concentrate in four places: (1) the landing/parse presentation layer (D1–D7) — the biggest qualitative loss; (2) exact values DESIGN.md deliberately delegates back to the mockup (D9), which undercuts its role as standalone contract; (3) lineage's visual language (D10–D11); (4) two genuine spec conflicts the spines create without resolving (D16 mono editor, D17 16px radius; plus the radial-gradient ban vs the mesh, D1). Nothing behavioral was dropped beyond the accepted demo-shortcut list. Reconciliation recommended before build: transcribe the delegated values, design the missing landing presentation, and resolve the three ban conflicts explicitly.
